Our new work "From State of THE ART to Human Prediction Algorithms" is now available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16464).
This is not an algorithms paper on computer vision, but rather a discussion of human intuition and ideas in current prediction algorithms, from the perspective of the interactive art that is isomorphic to them.

Project homepage: [https://cocoon2wong.github.io/sota](https://cocoon2wong.github.io/sota)
