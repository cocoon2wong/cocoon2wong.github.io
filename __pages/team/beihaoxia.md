## Statement

*(Posted on Feb 7, 2026)*

Conghao Wong has removed the personal curriculum vitae of Beihao Xia from Project Unpredictable in accordance with considerations of professional and academic ethics.

We affirm our commitment to established standards of scholarly conduct. Specifically, we do not assign authorship without substantive contribution, do not misuse institutional affiliations, do not misrepresent funding sources, do not request citations to our own work while serving as reviewers, and do not otherwise leverage academic positions in ways that conflict with accepted ethical norms.

Transparency is a foundational principle of Project Unpredictable. The project is guided by a commitment to responsible research practice and to the long-term integrity of the research community.

*(Updated on Oct 12, 2026)*

   Neither Conghao Wong nor Project Unpredictable has contributed to, supervised, reviewed, or authorized the work presented in *S3-Net*. This project is entirely independent of Project Unpredictable and does not represent an authorized continuation or collaborative outcome of our research.

These original figures were previously published in:
   - *"View vertically: A hierarchical network for trajectory prediction via fourier spectrums"* (ECCV 2022);
